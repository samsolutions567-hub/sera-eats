Our Vision

What sets DEMO.SAM apart from other brands?

DEMO.SAM stands out by offering fresh, customizable, and health-focused meals made with high-quality ingredients, quick service, and flavors tailored to modern lifestyles.

Are your ingredients organic and locally sourced?

Yes! We prioritize using organic, locally sourced ingredients whenever possible to ensure freshness and support sustainable farming practices.

Can I customize my salad or bowl?

Yes — you can customize your salad or bowl with your choice of greens, proteins, toppings, and dressings to suit your taste and dietary needs.

Do you offer vegan or gluten-free?

Yes — DEMO.SAM offers vegan and gluten-free options, including plant-based proteins, plenty of veggies, and dressings or ingredients that fit those dietary preferences. If you have specific dietary needs, you can always ask the staff to confirm which items meet your requirements.

How does delivery or pickup work?

You can order for delivery or pickup through DEMO.SAM’s app, website, or delivery partners. Simply choose your items, customize them, and select delivery to your location or pickup at your nearest store at a convenient time.

What’s the best way to store salad?

The best way to store salad is in an airtight container in the refrigerator, keeping greens dry and dressings separate to stay fresh and crisp longer.
